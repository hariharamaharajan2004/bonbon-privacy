In short: Bonbon is designed as an offline match-3 game. It does not require a Bonbon account, and core gameplay progress and preferences can be stored locally on your device.

1Information We Collect

Bonbon (“Bonbon”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a mobile match-3 puzzle game distributed through Google Play.

Bonbon does not require you to create an account or provide personal information directly to us in order to play the core game.

The Game does not intentionally request information such as your name, home address, phone number, email address, passwords, government identification numbers, contacts, photos, precise location, or health information.

2Local Game Data

Bonbon may store gameplay and preference information locally on your device so that the Game can remember your progress and settings.

This may include:

  • Completed and unlocked levels
  • Scores or star progress
  • Sound and music preferences
  • Haptic feedback preferences
  • Other game configuration settings

This information is used to provide normal Game functionality and is not intentionally transmitted to a developer-operated server by the Game.

Uninstalling the Game or clearing its application data may remove locally stored information.

3Internet Connectivity

Bonbon is designed to provide its core gameplay without requiring a continuous internet connection.

Internet connectivity may still be required for platform-level functions such as downloading, installing, updating, purchasing, or otherwise interacting with Google Play.

4Google Play and Third-Party Services

Bonbon is distributed through Google Play. Google and other platform providers may independently collect and process information in connection with their services.

Such processing may include information related to application downloads, installations, purchases, security, diagnostics, device compatibility, or Google Play activity.

Bonbon does not control Google's independent data practices. Users should review Google's applicable privacy policies and terms for information about those practices.

5Payments and Purchases

If Bonbon is offered as a paid application, the purchase is processed through Google Play's payment infrastructure.

Bonbon does not directly collect or store your credit card number, debit card number, bank account information, UPI credentials, payment passwords, or other complete payment credentials merely because you purchase the Game through Google Play.

6Advertising

The current version of Bonbon does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising purposes and does not require users to create advertising profiles for targeted advertising.

7Analytics

Bonbon is designed without requiring a user account for core gameplay.

The Game does not intentionally send locally stored gameplay progress, such as completed levels, scores, or local preferences, to a developer-operated analytics server.

Third-party platform services may independently collect technical or usage information under their own policies.

8Device Permissions and Features

Bonbon is designed to use only device functionality needed for the Game.

The Game does not intentionally request access to contacts, personal photographs, SMS messages, precise location, or microphone recordings for normal gameplay.

Bonbon may use standard device haptic/vibration functionality when haptic feedback is enabled.

Audio effects and music are part of normal gameplay and do not require microphone recording.

9Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with Bonbon. Because gameplay information is primarily stored locally, the security of that information is also affected by the security of the device itself.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10Data Retention

Bonbon's locally stored gameplay information generally remains on the device until it is deleted by the Game, removed through device settings, removed when the Game is uninstalled, or otherwise removed by the operating system or user.

Information independently retained by Google Play or other third-party services is governed by the respective provider's policies.

11Data Sharing

Bonbon does not intentionally sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Bonbon does not intentionally provide locally stored gameplay progress to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

Platform operators and service providers may independently process information necessary for application distribution, payment processing, security, compatibility, platform operation, or diagnostics.

12Children's Privacy

Bonbon is a general-audience puzzle game. The Game does not knowingly require children to provide personal information such as their name, address, phone number, or email address.

The Game does not require users to create an account to play.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of mobile applications and review the privacy practices of the platforms through which applications are obtained.

13Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Bonbon's features, platform requirements, applicable laws, or data practices change.

When changes are made, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page may be updated accordingly.

14Privacy Questions

If you have a privacy-related question concerning Bonbon, please use the developer contact method provided on Bonbon's Google Play store listing.

We may update this section if a dedicated privacy contact method is introduced in the future.

Bonbon at a glance: No Bonbon account is required for core gameplay. Gameplay progress and preferences can be stored locally. Core gameplay is designed to work offline. Google Play may independently process information when you download, purchase, update, or interact with the Game through its platform.
