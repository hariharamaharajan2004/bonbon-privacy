Bonbon — Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 5, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Bonbon (“Bonbon”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you download, install, access, or play the Bonbon mobile game (the “Game”).
Bonbon is a mobile match-3 puzzle game developed and distributed through Google Play. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the information associated with the Game.
By downloading or using Bonbon, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
1. Information We Collect
Bonbon is designed to operate primarily as an offline game and does not require you to create an account or provide personal information directly to us in order to play.
1.1 Personal Information
Bonbon does not directly request or require information such as:
- Your name
- Home address
- Phone number
- Email address
- Passwords
- Government identification numbers
- Contacts
- Photos or videos
- Precise location information
- Financial account information
- Health information
The Game does not contain a user registration or account creation system.
2. Game Progress and Local Storage
Bonbon may store certain gameplay information locally on your device so that your progress and preferences can be maintained between sessions.
This may include information such as:
- Completed levels
- Unlocked levels
- Star or score progress
- Gameplay settings
- Sound preferences
- Music preferences
- Haptic feedback preferences
- Other game configuration settings
This information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the normal functionality of the Game.
Bonbon uses device-local storage mechanisms for this purpose. This information is not intentionally transmitted to the developer's servers by the Game.
If you uninstall the Game or clear its application data, locally stored game information may be deleted.
3. Internet Connectivity
Bonbon is designed to provide its core gameplay functionality without requiring an active internet connection.
The Game does not require you to maintain an internet connection in order to play its core match-3 gameplay.
Certain functionality associated with the Google Play platform, including downloading, installing, updating, licensing, purchasing, or reviewing the Game, may require an internet connection and is controlled by Google Play and the applicable Google services.
4. Google Play Services
Bonbon is distributed through Google Play.
When you download or use an application through Google Play, Google may independently collect and process information according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.
For example, Google may process information relating to:
- Your Google account
- Device information
- Application installation information
- Purchases
- Payments
- Security and fraud prevention
- Application usage
- Crash or diagnostic information
- Google Play activity
Such information may be processed by Google independently of Bonbon.
Bonbon does not control Google's data collection practices.
For information about Google's handling of information, please review Google's applicable privacy documentation and Google Play policies.
5. Payments and Purchases
If Bonbon is distributed as a paid application, the purchase of the Game is processed through Google Play's purchasing and payment infrastructure.
Bonbon does not directly collect or store your:
- Credit card number
- Debit card number
- Bank account information
- UPI credentials
- Payment passwords
- Other payment credentials
Payment information is handled by the applicable payment provider, including Google Play and its payment infrastructure.
We do not have access to your complete payment credentials merely because you purchase the Game through Google Play.
6. Advertising
Bonbon does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising purposes.
The current version of Bonbon does not require users to create advertising profiles or provide personal information for targeted advertising.
If advertising functionality is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain the relevant data practices before or when such functionality becomes applicable.
7. Analytics
The current version of Bonbon is designed without requiring users to create accounts or provide personal information to use the core game.
Bonbon does not intentionally send your local gameplay progress, including your completed levels, scores, or locally stored preferences, to a developer-operated analytics server.
Third-party platform services, such as Google Play, may independently collect technical or usage information in accordance with their own policies.
8. Device Permissions
Bonbon is designed to require only permissions necessary for the Game's functionality.
The Game does not intentionally request access to:
- Your contacts
- Your camera
- Your microphone for recording
- Your SMS messages
- Your call history
- Your precise location
- Your personal photographs
- Your files for unrelated purposes
Some device-level functionality, such as vibration/haptic feedback, may be used when enabled by the Game's settings.
9. Audio and Haptic Feedback
Bonbon may provide sound effects, music, and haptic feedback as part of gameplay.
Audio settings may be stored locally on your device.
Haptic feedback may use the device's standard vibration/haptic functionality.
Bonbon does not use the microphone to record conversations or ambient audio as part of normal gameplay.
10. Children's Privacy
Bonbon is a general-audience puzzle game.
The Game does not knowingly request children to provide personal information such as their name, address, phone number, or email address.
The Game does not require users to create an account to play.
Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of mobile applications and should review the privacy practices of the platform through which applications are obtained.
If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.
11. Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with Bonbon.
Because Bonbon primarily stores gameplay information locally on the user's device, the security of that locally stored information is also affected by the security of the device itself.
Users should maintain appropriate security measures on their devices, including device lock mechanisms, operating-system updates, and other security controls provided by their device manufacturer.
No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
12. Data Retention
Bonbon's locally stored gameplay information generally remains on the user's device until it is:
- Deleted by the Game;
- Removed through the device's application-data controls;
- Removed when the Game is uninstalled; or
- Otherwise removed by the operating system or user.
We do not maintain a developer-operated database containing individual users' Bonbon gameplay progress.
Information independently retained by Google Play or other third-party services is governed by the respective service provider's policies.
13. Data Sharing
Bonbon does not intentionally sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.
Bonbon does not intentionally provide users' locally stored gameplay progress to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.
Third-party service providers and platform operators may independently process information in connection with services such as:
- Application distribution
- Payment processing
- Application security
- Device compatibility
- Platform operation
- Crash or diagnostic services
Where third-party processing occurs through the platform, the applicable third party's privacy policy governs that processing.
14. Third-Party Services
Bonbon is distributed through third-party platforms such as Google Play.
Third-party services may have their own terms, privacy policies, technologies, and data practices.
We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party services they use in connection with Bonbon.
Bonbon's Privacy Policy does not replace the privacy policies of Google or other third-party providers.
15. Offline Gameplay
One of Bonbon's primary design goals is to support offline gameplay.
Core game functionality, including playing levels and using locally stored game progression, is designed to operate without requiring continuous internet connectivity.
Disabling internet connectivity may prevent platform-level functions such as downloading updates, checking application availability, accessing Google Play services, or completing purchases through Google Play.
16. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect:
- Changes to Bonbon;
- Changes to applicable laws or regulations;
- Changes to platform requirements;
- Introduction or removal of features;
- Changes in our data practices; or
- Improvements to privacy and security practices.
When this Privacy Policy is updated, the Effective Date at the beginning of the policy may be changed accordingly.
Users are encouraged to review this page periodically for the latest information.
17. Your Privacy Choices
Depending on the information involved, you may have control over information stored by Bonbon on your device.
You may generally:
- Uninstall Bonbon;
- Clear Bonbon's application data through your device settings;
- Disable sound or music through the Game's settings;
- Disable haptic feedback through the Game's settings; and
- Control applicable device and platform permissions through Android settings.
Because Bonbon does not require a user account, there is no Bonbon account that users need to create or manage in order to play the Game.
18. International Users
Bonbon may be made available to users in different countries and regions through Google Play.
Privacy and data-protection laws vary between jurisdictions.
Where applicable, users may have additional rights under local privacy laws concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or other processing of personal information.
The availability and scope of such rights may depend on the applicable law and circumstances.

20. Summary
In simple terms:
Bonbon is designed to be a straightforward offline puzzle game.
- No Bonbon account is required.
- Core gameplay works offline.
- Gameplay progress and settings can be stored locally on the device.
- Bonbon does not intentionally collect your contacts, photos, messages, precise location, or microphone recordings.
- Bonbon does not intentionally sell personal information.
